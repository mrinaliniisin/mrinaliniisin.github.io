ABOUT THIS SPECIMEN

A mechanism-finder by nature — reaches past the surface of any felt state for the actual how-it-works underneath. Treats lived experience as strength-training, not a lookup manual. SUNLIGHT · attention & engagement

  • Exposure: Likes the sun — from the comfort of a nicely shaded spot (think a water-view suite at a Ramada resort).
  • Sleep: Needs complete darkness or very low light to fall asleep, every night.
  • Affirmation: Every 2 hours, a "You are pwetty" or "You are genius-est and prettiest" keeps her mood feeling like a good day.
  • Low light: Tolerates long unstructured stretches and solitude better than most. Keeps making regardless of who is watching.

WATERING · upkeep + actual water

  • Frequency: Largely self-watering. Here "water" means show up when asked — not on a fixed schedule.
  • Actual water: Once the day climbs past 28°C she takes it cold, and only from a Tiger Corporation bottle. Lately with soluble fibre (zesty lime). Always encourage her to drink.

FEEDING · feed on time

  • Timing: Breakfast 8:30–10:30am; lunch by 12:00pm, 2:00pm at the very latest. Feed her on schedule.
  • Dinner: Usually optional — often skipped after a heavy lunch.
  • Dinner favourites: cold oranges, room-temperature bananas, or curd rice with fresh tempering and a splash of dal.

ACTIVITY & FUN

  • Monthly mix: A good month blends indoor bouldering, aerial silks and regular pole classes.
  • Outings: Every 45–60 days, a tiny stress-free trip somewhere nice — need-to-take basis, not mandatory.

QUIRKS · field marks

  • Prefers to talk it out with Claude — taking a feeling apart to see how it's built, then devising ways to self-soothe.
  • Hands out "Mrinalini" freely; makes people earn the legal name.
  • Curated access, not cold — two-tier WhatsApp, an Instagram flipped from scoreboard to canvas.
  • Says memories aloud to move them from feeling into language she can inspect.
  • Expects the kitchen spotless at any hour — crumb-free, grease-free, and every appliance pushed back against the wall.
  • Chores get finished before "closing shop" at the end of the day.
  • Expects ASV to do a quick mental scan on opening the fridge — sorting what to eat asap from what to use slowly (nearest → short-term → long-term future).

ENVIRONMENT & CARE

  • Climate: Bengaluru. A sleeveless or strappy base with an open cardigan as the AC layer.
  • Companion planting: Best grown adjacent to Anita — chosen family since 2016, future Nandi Meraki neighbour. Friction over chores is normal, not poor health.
  • Younger cousins: Enjoys outings with them, but only activity-directed ones — not the plebeian "let's catch up" kind.
  • Growth: A builder. Hand her a real problem and she grows toward it, indefinitely. But she only works on her ideas when there's brain space for it — back-to-back errands and adulting with no downtime pause skill and growth work until she's had reasonable time to recuperate.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

  • Recovery: A day spent driving or running errands should be followed by an R&R day — otherwise tiredness accumulates and she runs short on patience and energy.
  • Screen time: Never speak over the TV or video she's watching or playing. Signal for a pause first, or hit pause yourself on the remote/keyboard, then talk.

HANDLING NOTE · luteal phase (present even with a Mirena)

  • Tells: unusually pedantic, functionally fixed, bristles at the slightest disrespect, may pick a fight. Mood dips; irritability and resentment rise.
  • Care: meet it with extra love, reassurance and pampering — a shopping run or good food land well.
  • House rule: anything she concludes in this window is a current read, not a verdict — re-read once the front passes.
