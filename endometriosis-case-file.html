Timeline of major events

  1. 31 Aug 2020Mon

    First consultation for severe period pain

    Blood tests and a pelvic scan the same day. The scan shows a small haemorrhagic cyst on the left ovary. Duphaston prescribed for three months.

    Dr Amrita Rao
  2. 11 Nov 2020Wed

    Endometriosis first suspected

    Vaginal ultrasound reports a right-ovary cyst, queried as haemorrhagic or endometriotic (4.3 cc). Treated with tablets. No further scan for 17 months.

  3. 17 months without a scan; tablets stopped after Feb 2021, pain back from Jan 2022
  4. 22 Apr 2022Fri

    Endometriotic cysts confirmed in both ovaries

    Right ovary cyst of 104 cc with recent bleeding; three to four cysts on the left; ovaries adherent to each other and to the uterus. Milky breast secretion and post-coital bleeding also reported at this visit. A week later, Cabgolin to lower prolactin and a four-month progestin course, twice a day.

    Dr Amrita Rao
  5. 23 Aug 2022Tue

    3D scan shows deep disease

    Right cyst halved to 59 cc, left cysts slightly larger. Kissing ovaries, bowel loops adherent to the uterus, rectosigmoid deep infiltrating endometriosis. CA-125 at 102.9. Post-coital bleeding treated again at the August visits.

  6. 25 Aug 2022Thu

    Surgery recommended

    Laparoscopic cystectomy, adhesiolysis and resection of bowel endometriosis, with a pre-anaesthesia check after tests. Egg freezing suggested. Dienogest to continue until surgery. Laparoscopy pencilled in for October 2022.

    Dr Amrita Rao
  7. 12 Sep 2022and 17 Oct

    Two Decapeptyl injections

    3.75 mg GnRH agonist, one month apart, to shrink the disease before surgery. ₹7,506 each. The 12 Sep prescription sets a review for 19 or 20 Oct; on its back, in the doctor's hand: "Consider – TAH & BSO", a hysterectomy with removal of both ovaries. On 17 Oct the plan is written as laparoscopic bilateral cystectomy, the October date has slipped, and a review is set for 28 Oct.

  8. 28 Oct 2022Fri

    Signed off for surgery

    Pre-anaesthesia evaluation cleared after the 24 Oct tests, ECG and chest X-ray. Blood count, kidney function, urine and infection screens all normal; haemoglobin up to 12.5 from 11.3 in April, and TSH down to 3.5 from 6.4 on the Thyronorm started in August. Operative consent signed the same day, with the risks to bowel, bladder and ureter explained.

    Dr G. Parameswara · Dr Amrita Rao
  9. 3 Nov 2022Thu

    Case handed to a new surgeon

    Dr Gayathri Karthik takes over, repeats the 3D scan and sets the date for 9 Nov. The scan finds the right cyst down to 18 cc after the injections, four cysts on the left, a 5 mm deep-infiltrating lesion on the right uterosacral ligament, adenomyosis along the back of the uterus, and the sigmoid and rectum stuck to the uterus and right ovary. Referred to Dr Sumit Talwar, general surgeon, for the cyclical rectal bleeding.

    Dr Gayathri Karthik · Dr Mala Sibal
  10. 4 Nov 2022Fri

    Bowel surgeon's opinion, surgery plan set

    Painful defecation, hard stools and rectal bleeding. Examination finds an anal fissure and skin tags; a colonoscopy is skipped as it would not change management. The possibility of a bowel resection and of a stoma is discussed, with a high-fibre diet and a laxative for now. That afternoon Dr Gayathri Karthik writes the plan as minimally invasive, robotic surgery for endometriosis with Dr Talwar assisting, and gives the bowel-prep instructions for 8 Nov.

    Dr Sumit Talwar · Dr Gayathri Karthik
  11. 9 Nov 2022admitted 8–11 Nov

    Laparoscopic surgery for endometriosis

    Under general anaesthesia. Diagnosis on discharge: bilateral endometriotic cysts with adenomyosis. Total bill ₹3,74,127, insurance paid ₹3,31,832, patient share ₹18,501. Lupride 11.25 mg given on 10 Nov.

    Dr Gayathri Karthik
  12. 18 Nov 2022Fri

    Stitches out, first post-op review

    The week before, 13 to 17 Nov, brought a cold, fever and sinusitis, and a thrombosis in the left hand, likely at the IV site, still persists. Follow-up care moves to Dr Aditi Godse. Lupride to continue.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  13. 19 Dec 2022Mon

    Fever a month after surgery

    Two days of fever at 100°F, headache and hip pain; COVID rapid test negative, chest clear, laparoscopy scars healthy. An infection screen with internal medicine the same day, from blood count and CRP to blood culture, dengue and malaria, costs ₹9,200.

    Dr Aditi Godse · Dr Sanjiv Rao
  14. 13 Jan 2023Fri

    Breakthrough bleed on Lupride

    A period arrives despite the GnRH agonist. Advised to ignore it and stay the course.

  15. 20 Feb 2023Mon

    Dienogest advised after surgery

    The January bleed is recorded as the last period. Plan: back on dienogest, 2 mg nightly for three months, then a scan. A urine culture from 9 Feb had grown E. coli. The notes in June show the dienogest was not started.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  16. Periods return on 10 Jun 2023, pain-free, about seven months after the single Lupride depot of 10 Nov
  17. 24 Jun 2023Sat

    Cycle back, dienogest planned

    Every later note lists one Lupride injection only, so the depot lasted about seven months rather than three. Meprate for ten days to bring on a period, then dienogest 2 mg daily from day one or two, for three months, with a scan after. Dienogest and Deviry bought the same day for ₹5,453.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  18. 19 Jul 2023Wed

    Dienogest started 8 Jul, spotting

    A scanty period on 7 Jul, dienogest from the next day, and staining since. Advised to watch it for a week and continue at two tablets a day, with a scan in three months.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  19. 26 Aug 2023Sat

    Bleeding continues on two tablets

    Twenty days on the doubled dose and still bleeding. Pelvic scan ordered.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  20. 2 Sep 2023Sat

    Follow-up scan, ten months after surgery

    Retroflexed uterus with adenomyosis along the back wall and a thin 4 mm lining. Right ovary stuck to the uterus for a short stretch; left ovary sitting in the pouch of Douglas, densely adherent, with a 1 cm endometriotic cyst. Turbid fluid and flimsy adhesions in the pouch, bowel adherent to the back of the uterus. Judged nothing to worry about. ₹4,250.

    Dr Mala Sibal
  21. 5 Sep 2023Tue

    Dienogest stopped, Mirena chosen

    Spotting since 31 Jul. Options laid out as tablets or a Mirena coil: Freedase for 21 days a cycle over six cycles is offered, but the patient is keen on the Mirena, to be fitted on day four to six of the next period. Egg freezing discussed again.

    Dr Aditi Godse
  22. 12 Sep 2023Tue

    Mirena inserted

    Period began 9 Sep. Fitted under ultrasound guidance, uterus normal size and anteverted, cavity 7 cm. The device bought the day before for ₹4,103; with the ₹6,000 insertion, consultation and an antispasmodic injection, ₹11,017 in all. Post-insertion tablets to about the end of September.

    Dr Aditi Godse

Figures are from the Manipal bills on file only. 7 of the consultations on file have no bill, so the true spend is likely a few thousand rupees higher.
