Desktop Setup

September 5, 2026

Over the years, I have experimented with a bunch of different (I mean really different) desk setups. My current one is by far the most satisfying simply because it is compact, mobile af! It took awhile to slowly undo my mistakes.

For example - I had a Macbook M2 which didn't allow for dual displays by default. So I upgraded to M4. I had bought an Ikea Bekant right angle table and that wouldn't go through the doors of my house. Then, I bought a straight version of the same table but forgot that the width of the table had to be below 80cms (the width of the door). Finally, when the time was right I was able to buy a more compact table which is the one I am using now.

And then the accessories followed - the clamp based shelf, the clamp based trays to hold drinks and snacks, a foot stool to help with long hours of sitting for day job work.

Lastly, I managed to bring my old sofa onto my current house and fit it with height adjustable wheels thus making the setup truly one of comfort. ah what peace and privilege it is to be here.
