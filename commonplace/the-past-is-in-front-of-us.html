The past is in front of us

The Aymara people, an indigenous group living in the Andes mountains of South America, visualize the past in front of them and the future behind them.

  • The Reason: They believe we can "see" the past because it has already happened and is known, while the future is unknown and unseen (literally behind our backs).
  • The Language: In the Aymara language, the word for past or front is nayra, which also translates to "eye" or "sight." The word for future or behind is q'ipa.
  • The Gestures: When Aymara speakers talk about past events, they gesture forward. When they talk about the future, they point or wave backward over their shoulders.
