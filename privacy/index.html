Privacy

This is a personal website. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms that collect anything about you.

YouTube likes mirror

The YouTube Likes page is filled in by a small program that reads my own liked videos through the YouTube Data API, using read-only access to my own Google account. It stores the list of video titles and links in this site's public repository and nothing else. It never reads anyone else's data, and it is used by nobody but me.

Payments

The pay page uses Razorpay to take contributions. Razorpay's checkout only loads once you press the pay button, and it is Razorpay, not this site, that handles your card, UPI, or bank details; I never see them. What I keep is the payment ID, the amount, and any note you typed, plus the email or phone number Razorpay passes back with a payment, so that a mistaken payment can be traced and refunded. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to what happens inside the checkout.

Analytics

Pages load a self-hosted, cookie-free analytics script that counts visits without identifying visitors.
